How it works

Pair once, then just throw.

  1. 1

    Pair with a code

    Open both apps on the same Wi-Fi. Your iPhone finds your Mac, and both show the same six-digit code. Confirm it on each and they trust each other from then on.

  2. 2

    Tell it where your Mac is

    Say whether your phone sits below, left of, right of or above your Mac, and pick the display. That edge becomes your throw direction.

  3. 3

    Flick a photo or file

    Choose something from Photos or Files and flick it toward that edge. A haptic tap confirms the throw, and it slides onto your Mac's screen from the matching side.

Private by design

Your files never leave your network.

There's no server in the middle and no account to make. Your iPhone talks directly to your Mac, and nothing gets saved until your Mac has checked every byte.

  • Only Macs you approved

    A device can't send or receive until you've matched its pairing code. Unknown devices are refused.

  • Encrypted on the wire

    Every transfer runs over an authenticated, encrypted connection with keys created during pairing.

  • Verified before it's saved

    Your Mac checks the file's SHA-256 fingerprint and only then moves it into your folder. Your iPhone shows "sent" only after the Mac confirms.

  • You choose the folder

    The Mac app writes only to the folder you picked. No quiet access to your Desktop or photo library.

Two small apps

One on your phone, one in your menu bar.

iPhone

  • Pick from Photos or Files
  • A throw surface with haptic feedback
  • A Send button if you'd rather tap
  • Live progress and clear errors with retry

Mac

  • Lives quietly in the menu bar
  • An arrival animation that never steals focus
  • A notification when the animation can't show, such as over full-screen apps
  • A transfer history you can clear, with an Open in Finder button

Supports VoiceOver, and follows Reduce Motion: you get a calm static confirmation in place of the flying animation.

FAQ

Good to know

Is this a replacement for AirDrop?

No. Spatial Bridge is a quicker, more visual way to move things to one Mac you use every day. Pair once and you never pick a destination again.

Do I need the internet?

No. Both devices need to be on the same local network. Some guest, hotel and office Wi-Fi blocks devices from finding each other, and so do some VPNs. The app tells you when that's happening.

Can I flick from inside the Photos app?

No. iOS doesn't let apps capture gestures inside other apps, so you pick photos and throw them from within Spatial Bridge.

How big can files be?

Up to 100 MB each, one at a time, in this first release. Keep the app open while it sends. If a transfer is interrupted, it starts again from the beginning and never leaves a half-saved file behind.

Does it know where my phone is?

No. You set the direction once, and it doesn't use sensors or location to guess. If you move your setup around, change the direction in Settings.

What do I need?

An iPhone on iOS 26 or later and a Mac on macOS 26 or later.
