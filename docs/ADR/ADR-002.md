# ADR-002: Bonjour for discovery, cryptography for identity

Status: Accepted

## Context
Bonjour names, IPs and TXT records are attacker-controlled on a LAN (AD §6 boundary 1).

## Decision
The Mac advertises `_spatialbridge._tcp` only while Available is on (FR-202). TXT carries `pid`, `name`, `plat`, `pv` as hints only. Identity is the Ed25519 key fingerprint verified during pairing and enforced by TLS-PSK on every session.

## Consequences
A spoofed Bonjour record can at worst cause a failed handshake (E004).
